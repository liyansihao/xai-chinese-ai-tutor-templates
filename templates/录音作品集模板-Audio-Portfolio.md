## Recommended Recording Setup

- Find a quiet indoor room.
- Use a phone, Mac microphone, or external microphone.
- Keep the microphone close to your mouth, but do not touch it.
- Record each sample separately.
- Speak naturally. Do not overperform.
- Do one short test recording before the final version.
- Listen back and check: volume, noise, clipping, unclear words, long silence.
- Export as `.mp3` or `.m4a`.

## Portfolio Methodology Statement

For audio annotation, I focus on five dimensions:

1. Transcript accuracy: whether the spoken content is captured faithfully.
2. Prosodic detail: pauses, emphasis, rhythm, intonation, and emotional tone.
3. Contextual judgment: whether the speaker's intent is clear from surrounding context.
4. Audio quality: background noise, clipping, volume consistency, and clarity.
5. Annotation consistency: whether the same type of speech event is labeled the same way across samples.

My goal is to produce annotations that are accurate, explainable, and useful for training AI systems to understand natural Chinese speech.
